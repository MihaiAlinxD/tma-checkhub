Romanian road vignette: validity checks and expiry tracking

How to keep track of Romania’s road-use vignette (rovinieta) and check validity before traveling.

Why validity matters

The Romanian road vignette must be valid when driving on roads where it is required. Check the rules and exemptions applicable to your route.

Which details to save

Keep the registration number, vehicle category, start date, expiry date and proof of purchase.

Check before traveling

Do not rely solely on a note. Confirm validity through official channels and carefully check the information entered when purchasing.

Keep deadlines together

Track your road vignette, RCA insurance, ITP inspections and servicing in one place instead of searching through different documents.
