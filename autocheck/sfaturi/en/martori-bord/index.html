Dashboard warning lights: meanings, causes and actions

Learn what common dashboard lights mean and what to do when they appear.

Symbols and colours vary by model. Illustrations are indicative. Consult your vehicle handbook; if you cannot continue safely, stop and seek assistance.

Oil pressure

Red · Combustion / hybrid

Indicative priority: High priority – respond safely

View details

Possible causes

Low oil, leaks, faulty pump or sensor.

What to do and can you continue?

Risks

Frequently asked questions

What do the colours mean?

Red may indicate urgency or a safety condition; amber may require inspection or indicate a function. Green and blue often indicate active functions. Follow the manual and displayed message.

Can I drive if a light flashes?

It depends on the symbol. If STOP appears, braking or steering is impaired or the engine runs poorly, stop safely and seek assistance.
