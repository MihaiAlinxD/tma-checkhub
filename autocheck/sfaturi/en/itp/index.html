ITP in Romania: tracking inspections and deadlines

Learn how to track the Romanian periodic technical inspection (ITP) and keep inspection records.

Where to find the ITP deadline

Check your vehicle documents and official inspection records. Inspection frequency depends on the vehicle category and characteristics.

What to check before your appointment

Ask an authorized inspection center about required documents and check your vehicle’s general condition, including lights, tires, brakes and other safety components. A preliminary check does not replace the official inspection.

Keep a record

Record the inspection date, next deadline and any repairs. This makes it easier to see when the vehicle was last inspected.

Use official sources

For validity periods and applicable requirements, consult the Romanian Automotive Register (RAR) and your vehicle documents.
