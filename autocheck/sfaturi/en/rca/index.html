RCA insurance: tracking expiry dates and organizing documents

Keep your Romanian compulsory motor insurance policy, expiry date and vehicle documents organized.

Why tracking the expiry date matters

Your Romanian compulsory motor insurance (RCA) policy has a defined validity period. Check the exact date on your policy and plan renewal in advance. Do not assume it renews automatically.

What to keep on record

Record the vehicle, insurer, policy reference, coverage dates and where the document is stored. Keep an accessible copy as appropriate.

An easier way to stay organized

Keep separate records for each vehicle and review your documents regularly. AutoCheck helps you organize vehicle information and expiry dates.

Check official information

For policy status and legal requirements, rely on the issued documents and relevant official sources. Rules can change.
